## DNA BISNIS – DELIVRY

---

### 1. **Core Identity**

* **Nama:** DELIVRY
* **Kategori:** Super App Mobility & On-Demand Services
* **Model:** Multi-service platform (ride-hailing + delivery + commerce)
* **Tagline:** *Move Anything Anytime*
* **Value Proposition Utama:** Kecepatan, fleksibilitas, dan integrasi layanan dalam satu aplikasi

---

### 2. **Problem → Solution Fit**

#### Problem:

* Transportasi tidak efisien (waktu tunggu lama)
* Pengiriman barang tidak fleksibel (jam terbatas, mahal)
* Fragmentasi layanan (ride, food, kurir terpisah)
* Kurangnya transparansi harga & tracking

#### Solution:

* Platform tunggal untuk:

  * Ride (motor/mobil)
  * Delivery (barang/makanan)
  * On-demand services
* Real-time tracking
* Dynamic pricing
* Sistem driver terdistribusi (gig economy)

---

### 3. **Target Market**

#### Primary:

* Urban & semi-urban users (18–45 tahun)
* Pengguna aktif smartphone
* UMKM (butuh pengiriman cepat)

#### Secondary:

* Driver/freelancer (mitra pengemudi)
* Merchant (resto, toko, retail lokal)

---

### 4. **Core Services (Modular System)**

* **Ride**

  * Motor (fast, low cost)
  * Mobil (comfort)

* **Delivery**

  * Instant courier
  * Same-day delivery

* **Food**

  * Order makanan
  * Integrasi merchant lokal

* **Send / Package**

  * Kirim barang kecil–besar

* **Mart (Future Expansion)**

  * Grocery / retail delivery

---

### 5. **Business Model**

#### Revenue Streams:

* Komisi dari driver (10–25%)
* Komisi dari merchant (15–30%)
* Biaya layanan pengguna (service fee)
* Surge pricing (peak hours)
* Iklan & placement merchant
* Subscription (future: premium user)

---

### 6. **Operational Engine (Logic System)**

#### Matching Algorithm:

* Driver terdekat
* Rating driver
* Traffic & ETA
* Load balancing driver

#### Pricing Engine:

* Base fare
* Distance + time
* Surge multiplier
* Demand vs supply ratio

#### Order Flow:

1. User input tujuan / order
2. System calculate harga + ETA
3. Broadcast ke driver
4. Driver accept
5. Tracking real-time
6. Completion + payment

---

### 7. **User Experience Principles**

* 3 klik maksimal untuk order
* UI minimal, cepat, jelas
* Fokus ke:

  * Speed
  * Visibility (tracking)
  * Trust (rating & review)

---

### 8. **Brand Positioning**

* **Tone:** Modern, cepat, tegas
* **Visual:** Ungu → premium + tech + trust
* **Persona:**

  * “Cepat tanpa ribet”
  * “Semua bisa dikirim”

---

### 9. **Competitive Strategy**

#### Lawan:

* Gojek
* Maxim
* Grab

#### Diferensiasi:

* Lebih ringan (app size kecil)
* Fokus ke daerah underserved (non kota besar)
* Harga lebih agresif
* Sistem driver lebih fleksibel (fee rendah)

---

### 10. **Growth Engine**

#### Supply Growth:

* Rekrut driver lokal
* Insentif onboarding
* Sistem referral

#### Demand Growth:

* Promo diskon
* Partnership UMKM
* Campaign lokal

---

### 11. **Scalability Structure**

* Microservices backend
* Modular feature (ride, food, delivery bisa dipisah)
* Cloud-based infra
* API-first architecture

---

### 12. **Long-Term Vision**

* Super app regional (Indonesia Timur → nasional)
* Integrasi:

  * Fintech (wallet, paylater)
  * Logistics B2B
  * SaaS untuk UMKM

---

### 13. **Key Metrics (KPI)**

* Daily Active Users (DAU)
* Completed Orders / day
* Driver Utilization Rate
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Customer Retention
* CAC vs LTV

---

### 14. **Risk & Critical Points**

* Supply driver tidak stabil
* Burn rate tinggi (promo)
* Kompetitor besar (pricing war)
* Infrastruktur jaringan di daerah

---

### 15. **Strategi Eksekusi Awal (Phase 1)**

* Launch 1 kota kecil / menengah
* Fokus:

  * Ride + Delivery dulu
* Bangun:

  * 100–300 driver aktif
  * 50+ merchant
* Validasi demand sebelum scale
